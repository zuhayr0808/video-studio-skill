# Short-form video guide

Read this for TikTok, Reels and Shorts.

## Format

- 9:16 vertical, 1080x1920, 30 or 60 fps, H.264 video with AAC audio, MP4 file.
- Keep important text out of the top 15% (search bar and header) and the bottom 20% (caption and profile overlay). Also keep the right edge clear, where the buttons sit.
- Target 15 to 30 seconds unless the user asks for more. Shorter loops rewatch better.

## The first three seconds

- The hook is on screen and understood by second 3, with the sound off. Measure its text size and contrast.
- Frame 0 is never black or blank. Start on the action.
- The spoken hook is a punchy fragment. Do not open with greetings like "Hey guys".
- On-screen text should add to the spoken words, not repeat them.
- Change something visual about every 1.5 to 2 seconds in the opening.

## Structure for 15 to 30 seconds

1. 0 to 3 s: the hook.
2. 3 to 10 s: the problem or the curiosity gap.
3. 10 to 20 s: the payoff, shown and not only told.
4. Last 2 to 3 s: loop back so the end flows into the opening line or image.

## Sound

- Research first. Search for what is working now and record the source for each sound or format. If you cannot confirm it, label it unverified.
- Use only royalty-free or licensed audio. Log the license.
- Keep music under the voice. Run the audio check and report the real loudness and any silences over half a second.
- Match cuts and text pops to the beat when the music has a clear tempo. Measure the tempo instead of guessing it.

## Writing style

- Talk like a person. Use contractions and concrete numbers.
- Cut corporate words such as leverage, game-changer and streamline.

## Checklist before delivering

- [ ] Hook visible by 3 s with sound off
- [ ] Frame 0 not black
- [ ] Text inside the safe zones and readable at phone size
- [ ] Loop seam checked
- [ ] Audio measured, voice clear
- [ ] Every sound has a source and a license
- [ ] Anything unconfirmed is marked unverified
